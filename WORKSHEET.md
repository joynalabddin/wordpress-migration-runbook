## 5. WordPress destination readiness

- [ ] Supported WordPress, PHP, plugins, themes, and hosting are selected.
- [ ] HTTPS and a protected staging environment are working.
- [ ] Backup and restoration procedures are available.
- [ ] Permalink structure is chosen after comparing old Blogger URLs.
- [ ] Users, categories, tags, menus, forms, security, and analytics are configured.
- [ ] Search Console, sitemap, canonicals, robots rules, and redirects have an owner.

## 7. Launch and post-launch review

- [ ] Fresh WordPress backup is stored before launch.
- [ ] Original Blogger content and XML remain available during testing.
- [ ] DNS or domain change has an approved maintenance window.
- [ ] Redirects, 404s, canonicals, sitemap, robots, and HTTPS are tested.
- [ ] Search Console, analytics, forms, uptime, backups, and security monitoring are connected.
- [ ] Crawl errors, indexing, traffic, media, broken links, and user reports are reviewed after launch.
- [ ] Known issues, fixes, rollback status, and next steps are documented.
